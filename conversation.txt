| Target | Timeline | Details |
|--------|----------|---------|
| **Renewable Energy Share** | 2030 | **42.5% of EU energy** from renewables |
| **With effort** | 2030 | **45% possible goal** |
| **Heating & Cooling** | 2030 | **23% from renewables** |
| **Transport** | 2030 | **14.5% renewable** (biofuels, e-fuels, electricity) |

## **RED IV'S REVOLUTIONARY ARTICLES FOR COMMUNITIES**

### 🏘️ **Article 22: Renewable Energy Communities (REC)**

This is **HUGE** for Meerkracht!

**What it means:**
- Citizens can form legal entities to produce renewable energy
- Don't need to be energy companies
- Can be cooperatives, associations, SMEs
- Don't need to have energy as main business

**Rights of RECs:**
✅ **Generate renewable energy** for own use
✅ **Store energy** (batteries, thermal storage)
✅ **Sell excess energy** to the grid
✅ **Participate in energy markets**
✅ **Receive support** (subsidies, grants, loans)

### 🔄 **Article 23: Collective Self-Consumption**

**What it enables:**

Neighbors can **share renewable energy** they produce together, without:
- Operating a full energy company
- Paying transmission fees between neighbors
- Complex licensing

**How it works:**
```
Neighbor A (solar panels) 
        ↓
    Shared Battery
        ↓
Neighbor B (uses stored energy)
```

**Requirements:**
- Located in same building or nearby
- Connected through same grid point or close connection
- Must be "active consumers" (produce & consume)

**Meerkracht potential:** Perfect for Meerstad's neighborhood layout!

## **1. THE GAS EXTRACTION LEGACY** ⚠️

- **Decades of natural gas extraction** (since 1959)

**Problems this creates:**
- ❌ **Thousands of homes still on gas** - need to switch to alternatives
- ❌ **Gas infrastructure aging** - expensive to maintain or replace
- ❌ **Economic dependency** - region lost major income source
- ❌ **Just transition concerns** - workers, communities need support
- ❌ **Consumer affordability** - switching costs high for residents

---

## **2. HEATING THE REGION WITHOUT GAS** 🔥

### The Core Challenge:
Groningen is **cold** (northern Netherlands) and **historically relied on gas heating**

**Current situation:**
- ~70% of Groningen homes still use **natural gas heating**
- Gas boilers must be replaced by heat pumps or alternatives
- **Heat pump challenge:** Very cold winters need powerful systems

### Specific Problems:

**a) Heat Infrastructure Gap**
- ❌ **No district heating network** in most areas (unlike cities like Amsterdam, Rotterdam)
- ❌ Building district heating from scratch = very expensive
- ❌ Individual heat pumps less efficient in extreme cold

**b) Electricity Grid Overload**
- If everyone switches to electric heat pumps simultaneously:
  - Grid demand in winter peaks dramatically
  - Current grid infrastructure **not ready**
  - Massive investment needed in grid reinforcement

**c) Cost Barriers**
- Heat pump installation: €8,000-15,000+ per home
- Insulation improvements needed first (another €20,000-40,000)
- **Total per household:** €30,000-60,000 is prohibitive for many

---

**Groningen has:**
- Older housing stock (pre-1980s construction)

- ❌ **Energy inefficiency** - high heating demand
